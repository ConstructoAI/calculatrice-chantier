1. Overview

The Constructo AI calculator works the way people talk on a jobsite: in feet, inches and fractions, as well as in metres and millimetres. It brings five things together.

  • The framer's keypad: pitches and rafters, hips, valleys and jack rafters, stairs, rake walls and arched walls, arcs and circles, compound mitres, material quantities, conversions and costs.
  • The keypad of your trade: [[NOMBRE_D_OUTILS]] tools for [[NOMBRE_DE_METIERS]] other trades. Tap your trade above the keypad, and the top keys become those of its tools.
  • The calculation tape, which keeps a record of everything that was entered and calculated, so you can review it, save it or share it.
  • The room sketch: a room's plan drawn with your finger, its measurements in feet, inches and fractions, its area and that of its walls, its doors, windows, partitions and furniture, in 3D, as a PDF and as a DXF.
  • The notepad, for your jobsite notes, with their photos and attached files.

The calculator is free, with no ads and no account. Nothing is sent over the Internet: your settings, memories, tapes, plans, notes and their photos stay on your device.

Disclaimer. This calculator is a calculation aid. It does not replace the drawings, the judgment of a qualified person, or the requirements of the building code in force where you work (in Québec, the Construction Code) and of municipal regulations. Always check your results before cutting or ordering.

The trade tools do geometry, physics and arithmetic. They do not reproduce any code table (ampacities, minimum wire sizes, minimum slopes, permitted fill) and contain no prices: you enter your own costs and rates.

2. Installing and opening Contents

On the Web: computer, tablet, phone

Open constructoai.github.io/calculatrice-chantier. The calculator runs as is, in the browser.

Installing it on a phone or tablet

Once installed, the calculator has its own icon, named Calculs, opens full screen and works offline, even at the far end of a basement.

  • Android (Chrome): open the site, then the calculator's ☰ menu, and tap Install the app. Or, in Chrome's menu: Install app.
  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): open the site, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): the calculator's ☰ menu, Install the app, or the install icon in the address bar.

Updates arrive on their own: each time it opens with a network connection, the calculator picks up the latest version. The current version is written at the bottom of the ☰ menu.

Where the system offers them — on Android, with a long press on the icon; on a computer, with a right-click on the taskbar icon —, the installed app has shortcuts to the trades, the drawing, the notepad and the tapes.

On Windows, without a browser

Download calculatrice-chantier-windows-x64.exe from the latest release, then run it: there is nothing to install. The executable is not signed; the first time you run it, Windows may display "Windows protected your PC": choose More info, then Run anyway.

In the Web version, clearing the site's browsing data also erases your settings, your saved tapes, your drawings, your notes and their photos. Nothing moves from one device to another: export what matters.

To keep your data safe, or move it to another device, from the phone to the computer: ☰ menu, Export my data, which gathers it all into one file; on the other device, Import data reads it and replaces its data, after confirmation.

4. Entering a measurement and calculating Contents

Measurements

Enter a measurement the way you say it: the number, then the unit.

To enterKeys
5 ft 7 1/2 in5 Feet 7 Inch 1 / 2
3/4 in3 / 4
7.3 m7 . 3 m
centimetres, millimetresthe number, then Conv 7 (cm) or Conv 9 (mm)
square feet, cubic feetthe number, then Feet twice, or three times
a weightthe number, then Conv 4 (lb), Conv 1 (kg), Conv 6 (short ton) or Conv 3 (metric tonne)

If you do not type the denominator of a fraction, the current resolution is used: 15 / means 15/16. The ⌫ key erases the last character typed.

Operations

The + − × ÷ and = keys work as on any calculator, with measurements as well as with numbers. Calculations chain from left to right.

Example 5 Feet 7 Inch 1 / 2 + 18 Feet 8 Inch = → 24 ft 3 1/2 in

A length times a length gives an area; an area times a length gives a volume.

Example 40 Feet × 35 Feet = → 1,400 ft²

Percentages

+ 10 % adds 10% to the value — your waste, in one move. − 20 % takes 20% off, × 18 % calculates 18% of it, ÷ 80 % divides it by 0.80.

Conversions

With a value displayed, press the unit you want: m converts it to metres, Feet to feet, Conv 9 to millimetres. Feet toggles between feet-inches and decimal feet, Inch between fraction and decimals.

The Conv key

Conv is the conversion and second-function key. Followed by a key, it runs the function written in small type above that key; followed by a unit, it converts the displayed value. You can also tap the small label directly.

Clearing

  • Clear, once: clears the display and any error.
  • Clear, twice: also clears the temporary values (rise, run, length, diameter…). Do this before a new problem. The entered pitch, the memories and the stored values are kept.
  • Conv × (Clear all): restores the calculator to new, after confirmation — memories, tape, stored values and the fields of the trade tool. Preferences are kept.

Copying and pasting

Double-clicking the displayed value copies it (or Ctrl+C). Ctrl+V pastes a number or a length written out: 12 ft 6 in, 30 in, 3.8 m.

5. Memories Contents

  • The cumulative memory. Each press of M+ adds the displayed value to the total; Conv M+ subtracts it. Recall M+ shows the total; M+ after that shows the average, then the number of values. Recall Recall shows the total and clears the memory.
  • Three free registers. Store 1, 2 or 3 stores the displayed value; Recall 1, 2 or 3 brings it back.
  • The calculator's stored values. Store 4 to 9 and 0 set the block, the on-centre spacing, the footing section, the target riser, the floor thickness, the target tread and the density. They can also be changed in preferences.
  • Recalling what a key holds in memory. Recall followed by a function key shows its last value: Recall Run shows the last run.

6. The framing keypad Contents

This is the basic keypad, the one the calculator shows when the trade bar reads Framing. Its function keys all follow the same rule: a value typed, then the key, stores the value; the key alone calculates. Press a key again to cycle through all its results.

Example A rafter: 7 Inch Pitch · 8 Feet 5 Inch Run · Diag → 9 ft 8 15/16 in, then Hip Valley → 12 ft 10 1/2 in

The top keys solve the right triangle: enter two values among Pitch, Rise, Run and Diag, then press the third. The pitch stays in memory: no need to enter it again for the next rafter. To send a result to another function, press = first.

Each key and each second function has its own topic, with a worked example, in the directory of keys and functions below.

7. The keypad of your trade Contents

Above the keypad, the trade bar starts with a solid chip: the current trade (Framing, at first). To its right, a row of chips scrolls sideways.

  • Choosing your trade: tap it in the row, or tap the solid chip to open the Your trade list.
  • Changing tools: once the trade is chosen, the row shows its tools. One tap is all it takes.
  • Going back to framing: tap the solid chip, then Framing, roofing and stairs.

The calculator remembers the current tool: you find it again the next time you open it.

Filling in a tool

The tool's fields become the top keys. Above each key is written the content of its field; when the field is empty, you read there, in parentheses, the unit of a number typed without a unit: (ft), (in), (A).

  1. Type a value, then press the key of the field where you want to store it. A bare number takes the field's unit: 24 on a field in feet gives 24 ft 0 in.
  2. A dark key cycles through the options of a list: the angle of a fitting, the size of a conductor.
  3. Press Result. The calculation is recorded on the tape and the first result is displayed, with its rank: 2 of 3. Press again to see the next one.

Example A slab 24 ft by 20 ft, 4 in thick. Concrete trade, Concrete slab tool: 24 Feet Length · 20 Feet Width · 4 Inch Thickness · Result → 480 ft² (44.6 m²) · Result → 5.93 yd³ (4.53 m³) · Result → 6.52 yd³ (4.98 m³) to order, 10% waste included.

Example A 12 in offset with 45° fittings. Plumbing trade, Pipe offset tool: 12 Inch Offset · Result → 1 ft 5 in of travel · Result → 1 ft 0 in of run. For another angle, press the dark Angle key until you reach the angle you want.

Example The voltage drop of a 120 V, 15 A circuit, 100 ft long, in 14 AWG copper. Electrical trade, Voltage drop tool: 15 Current · 100 Feet Length · Result → 9.42 V · Result → 7.85% · Result → 110.6 V at the load.

Good to know

  • Recalling a field: its key, with nothing typed, displays its value.
  • Resetting a field to its starting value: Conv, then its key. All the fields: tap the Start over label above Result.
  • A value is missing? The display names it: MISSING: WIDTH.
  • Keep calculating: a displayed result is a value like any other. × 2 =, M+ and Store all use it.
  • From one tool to the next: the displayed value follows you when you change tools. The loose volume of a trench, for example, goes into the Volume field of the truckloads tool with one press.
  • An area calculated on the keypad goes into a field in square feet: 40 Feet × 35 Feet =, then Area.
  • How to use a tool: press and hold one of its keys.

The same tools as forms

The Trades tab presents each tool as a form: all the fields and all the results at a glance, recalculated with every keystroke. A measurement is written out in full there (12 ft 6 in, 30 in, 3.8 m). The Add to tape button records the calculation; On keypad plugs the tool into the keys of the home screen. The form and the keypad share the same values.

Results follow the system of the first length you entered: in feet-inches or in metric for lengths, in both systems for areas, volumes and weights. Quantities to buy (bags, boxes, sheets) are rounded up to the next whole unit.

Pin, at the bottom of a tool's form, puts it at the head of the Trades tab and of the home screen's trade bar, marked with a star: a tap on its chip plugs it into the keypad. Unpin removes it; up to twelve tools can be pinned. The last tools you opened are found under Recent, at the head of the Trades tab.

Add to shopping list, below the results of a tool that gives bags, boards, boxes or rolls, writes them into the Shopping list note of the notepad, one line to tick per item, with its format: [ ] 24 × Bags of premixed concrete · Yield per bag: 14 L. An item already on the list adds up there as long as its line is not ticked.

PDF, below the results, makes a one-page report of the calculation — the date, the project, the inputs and the results, with the tool's note — to attach to a quote or to keep on file. The project, optional, is typed when exporting and stays offered for the next report.

All the tools are described, with an example, in the directory of tools by trade.

The cut list

The Cut list card, in the Trades tab, fits pieces into the stock lengths sold in stores: enter each piece, its quantity and its length (+ Piece adds a line), the stock lengths (8; 10; 12; 16 feet to start with) and the saw kerf (1/8 in). It gives the lengths to buy, the length bought and the offcut, then the cutting plan of each length, drawn to scale: the plan that buys the least length. For lumber, mouldings, conduit and pipe.

Example 4 pieces of 4 ft and 2 of 6 ft 6 in, from 8, 10, 12 or 16 ft → 2 × 16 ft 0 in, each cut into 6 ft 6 in + 4 ft + 4 ft, offcut 1 ft 5 5/8 in

Hours calculation

Choose Hours in the trade bar, or in the list the trade opens: the h and min keys are added at the top of the keypad, and hours add up like feet and inches. A number left after the hours counts as minutes; h on a result switches it to decimal hours, and back. The Timesheet, in the bar or in the Trades tab, counts the week's hours: arrival, departure and lunch each day, on the 24-hour clock; no pay rate.

Example 7 h 45 min + 8 h 30 min = → 16 h 15 min, then h → 16.25 h

The levelling book

The Levelling book card, in the Trades tab, keeps a laser level's book: the benchmark elevation and the reading taken on it give the height of instrument; each point's reading gives its elevation and, against the target elevation — the book's or the line's own —, what to cut or fill. The book is kept on the device, goes into the full backup and exports as CSV for a spreadsheet.

Example Benchmark 100 ft, read at 5 ft; a point read at 6 ft 3 in, target 99 ft → Elevation 98 ft 9 in · Fill 3 in

The level

On a phone or tablet with a tilt sensor, the Level card in the Trades tab turns it into a level: lay the device flat on a rafter, a pipe or a ramp, screen up; the bubble shows the high side, and the screen gives the slope in degrees, in 12 and in percent. Send puts a value on the calculator's display; Hold keeps the reading; Calibrate, on a level reference surface, makes it zero. On a device without a sensor, the card is hidden.

Example The phone on a 6/12 rafter → 26.57°, 6.00 in 12, 50.0%

8. The room sketch Contents

The Sketch tab makes the plan of a room: draw its outline with your finger or the mouse, enter the measurement of each side, and the calculator gives its area, its perimeter and the area of its walls. The drawing is a sketch: it does not need to be to scale.

Drawing the room

  • With your finger or the mouse, in one stroke or one side at a time: each stroke straightens to horizontal, vertical or 45°. The shape closes on its own when the drawing comes back to its start; Close the shape adds the missing side.
  • With the arrows at the bottom of the board: each one adds a side in its direction; the 45° button turns them diagonal.
  • Another angle: tap the side; below the keypad, Angle from the previous side sets it to 22.5°, 30°, 45°, 60° or 90°, or to the angle you type. The other sides keep their direction, and the worked-out ones follow. A partition segment likewise takes its angle from the previous segment.
  • Zooming: the + and − buttons on the board, two fingers or the mouse wheel; Fit reframes. A closed shape pans with one finger.
  • Undo goes back up to 40 steps, and Redo brings back what it undid; Clear all starts the room over.

Entering the measurements

Tap a side: the keypad opens below the drawing, with Feet, Inches, fractions of an inch and m. A number alone is in feet; Next moves to the next side without a measurement. As soon as only one side is missing in a direction, it is worked out from the others and shown in grey. Once everything is known, the shape is redrawn to scale and the screen gives the area and the perimeter, and, for a rectangle, the squaring diagonal to check on site.

Example A rectangle: 12 Feet on top, 10 Feet 6 Inches on the right → 126 ft² (11.7 m²), perimeter 45 ft 0 in, diagonal 15 ft 11 5/16 in

Example A rectangle of 12 Feet on top by 8 Feet on the left, its right side set to 60° → 9 ft 2 7/8 in and 16 ft 7 7/16 in worked out, area 114 ft²

An out-of-square room

A four-sided room is not always square. Measure its four sides, then its diagonals corner to corner: the Diagonals section offers 1 → 3 and 2 → 4, and the chosen diagonal shows dashed on the plan, with the corner numbers (corner 1 is the start of the first side). One diagonal is enough: the room is redrawn as it really is, and the screen gives each corner's angle and how far it is out of square, measured 4 ft from the corner (1 m in metric). With both, it also flags that they do not agree, beyond 1/4 in: measure them again.

Example A room of 12 Feet by 10 Feet, diagonal 1 → 3 of 15 Feet 7 Inches instead of 15 ft 7 7/16 in → corners of 90.3° and 89.7°, each 1/4 in over 4 ft

Several rooms and deductions

+ Room adds a room (30 at most); Rename, or a tap on the chosen room, changes its name. Duplicate adds a copy of it, right after it, and Mirror flips it, left and right swapped, with its measurements, openings, partitions and furniture: the other side of a semi-detached house is drawn in two taps. Deductions — island, stairwell, column — are rectangles taken off the area: the screen then gives the net area, then the total of the rooms.

Walls, doors and windows

  • The wall height, entered in the Walls section, gives the wall area, less that of the doors and windows.
  • + Door (2 ft 8 in × 6 ft 8 in) and + Window (3 ft × 4 ft): then tap their wall to place them, and drag them along the wall. Their size, their sill and their distance from the corner are set with the keypad.
  • Thickness: 9 in for exterior walls and 5 in for partitions, to start with. The Measurements: Interior | Exterior button tells which side of the walls your measurements are on.
  • Stretching a wall: drag the handle at its middle; its neighbours lengthen or shorten, and the measurements are rewritten.

Partitions, stairs, furniture and notes

  • + Partition: drag on the plan from one end to the other, turning corners as needed, for an L- or U-shaped partition. Its ends reach the nearby walls; to change a segment's length, tap it on the plan, or tap its length in the partition's row: the keypad opens below the plan, as for a wall, and the page scrolls back up to it on its own (del erases the old measurement; Next moves to the next segment), and the + Door on its row gives it a door.
  • Stairs and furniture — bed, table, sofa, counter, toilet, bathtub, shower, washer, fridge, range: tap its button, then the room to set it down, and drag it. +15° and +90° turn it; its size is set with the keypad. Stairs have a shape (straight, L-shaped, U-shaped, quarter-turn, spiral), a width, a length and a rise; their risers are worked out at 7 1/2 in at most, and the tread of straight stairs follows. Furniture does not overlap: when moved, an item stops against the others, against partitions and against walls, and slides along them.
  • Copy, on the row of an item or a partition, sets a copy beside it: the item in the nearest free spot, the partition offset by a foot, to drag into place. The Mirror of a turning staircase (L-shaped, U-shaped, quarter-turn, spiral) makes it turn the other way.
  • + Note: tap the spot on the plan, then type the note.

3D, the tape, the tools and the exports

  • The 3D button shows the room in 3D, with its openings, partitions and furniture: drag to turn around it; 2D goes back to the plan.
  • Add to tape records the measurements and results on it. Send to a tool… opens a trade tool (slab, pavers, excavation…) with a rectangle of the same area and the same perimeter as the room.
  • Export as PDF makes a Letter-size document, one page per room; Export as DXF, a full-scale drawing for AutoCAD, LibreCAD or QCAD.

My drawings, from one device to another

The My drawings button, at the top of the screen, keeps several drawings — a house, a cottage, a basement —, each with its rooms. + New drawing starts one; Open, Rename and Delete do what they say; Duplicate copies a drawing, to compare two variants. To pick up a drawing on another device, from the phone to the computer or the other way around: Save as a file makes a file of it, which you send by email, through a cloud or with a cable; on the other device, Open a file… reopens it.

Your drawings stay on the device and are there the next time you open the calculator. Walls are drawn square or at 45°, then take any angle if needed.

9. The calculation tape Contents

The tape keeps a record of your calculations: the operations and their totals, the values given to functions and their results, the conversions, and the details of each trade tool calculation, under its title.

  • Viewing it: the Tapes tab, or Conv =. In a wide window, it stays on screen to the right of the calculator.
  • Saving: give it a name — the client's or the jobsite's. Saved tapes can be reopened, renamed, copied, exported and deleted from the Tapes tab.
  • Copying: the text of the tape goes to the clipboard, ready to paste into an email or a text message.
  • Exporting: a .txt file.
  • Clearing: erases the current tape, after confirmation.

The current tape keeps the last 500 lines. Clear twice draws a separator line on it; Clear all empties it.

10. The notepad Contents

The Notes tab keeps your jobsite notes on the device, from the most recent to the oldest.

  • Writing: + New note opens one. Its title and text save on their own; Back returns to the list, where a note left empty disappears.
  • Ticking: a line that starts with [ ] shows as a checkbox, above the text: a tap ticks it ([x]) or unticks it. + Checkbox adds one at the end of the text; the list tells how many are ticked.
  • Searching: the Search the notes field, at the head of the list, keeps only the notes whose title or text contains all the words typed, regardless of capitals and accents.
  • The shopping list: the Shopping list note receives the items of the calculators of the Trades tab; tick what is bought, then copy or export it for the supplier.
  • Copying, exporting, deleting: Copy puts the note on the clipboard, Export makes a text file of it, Delete erases it with its attachments, after confirmation.

Attached photos and files

Below a note's text, + Photo attaches photos — on a phone, the camera or the gallery — and + File attaches a file: a PDF, a plan, a quote. Photos appear as thumbnails, the right way up; tap one to see it full size. Save keeps a copy elsewhere (a download, in the browser), Remove erases it. On a computer, Open opens an attached file in its program.

Dimensioning a photo

On a photo seen full size, tap + Dimension, then drag from one end to the other of what you measured: a window, an opening, a wall. Enter the measurement taken with the tape, as in the sketch (a number alone is in feet). The first dimension is the reference: a dimension left empty is estimated from it and marked ~. The estimate is only right in the plane of the reference: perspective distorts the rest. Tap a dimension to choose it, drag its ends, or remove it; Export as PDF makes a PDF of the dimensioned photo.

The notepad keeps up to 500 notes, and each note up to 50 attachments. A photo larger than 2048 pixels is reduced to 2048 pixels to save space on the device; an attached file cannot exceed 25 MB. Photos are read in JPEG and PNG: any other image is attached as a file.

Job-site mode

For working outdoors, in full sun or with gloves: Job-site mode, in the menu or in the preferences, switches the calculator to black, white and safety yellow, flat and high-contrast. On the calculator screen, the bottom five rows — units, digits and operations — are taller, and the keypad goes down to the bottom of the screen, under your thumb, for one-handed typing; the menu then leads to the other screens. A pressed key turns inverse, easy to see in the sun. Leave job-site mode, in the menu, brings back the colours of the chosen theme.

The language

The FR | EN button in the top bar switches the whole calculator from French to English and back: the keys, the display, the tools, the help and this manual. The way numbers are written and the unit symbols follow the language: 1 234,5 pi² in French, 1,234.5 ft² in English. Your values do not change: what you typed in a tool is rewritten in the new language. The first time it opens, the calculator takes the language of the device if it is French or English; after that, it keeps the one you chose.

In English, the decimal separator is always the point, and a comma typed between groups of three digits separates thousands: 1,500 is one thousand five hundred. In French, the comma is always the decimal separator. Units, however, can be typed in either language: 12 pi 6 po as well as 12 ft 6 in.

14. Messages and troubleshooting Contents

The display showsWhat it means
NO VALUEThe key has nothing to recall or calculate: it is missing a value. Type it, then press again.
MISSING: …The trade tool is still waiting for the value of the field named.
PLEASE FIX: …The field named holds an entry the tool cannot read. Type its value again.
ERROR – unit mismatchThe units do not go together: a length added to a bare number, a length in a field that expects amperes. Press Clear.
ERROR – invalid entryThe value makes no sense here: zero where a measurement is needed, a decimal number where a whole number is needed. Press Clear.
ERROR – cannot calculateThe calculation has no result: a division by zero, an impossible triangle. Press Clear.
ERROR – trigonometryThe value is outside the function's domain: an inverse sine greater than 1, for example. Press Clear.
ERROR – overflowThe result is too large for the display. Press Clear.
  • A result looks rounded. Lengths are displayed at the fraction resolution chosen in preferences (to 1/16 of an inch, at first); the calculation itself keeps its full precision.
  • A framing function gives an unexpected result. A value from an earlier calculation may still be in memory: press Clear twice and start again.
  • The framing keys have disappeared. A trade tool is on the keypad: tap the trade chip, then Framing, roofing and stairs.
  • The calculator is in the wrong language. Tap FR or EN in the top bar.
  • The installed calculator does not have the latest version. Open it once with a network connection, close it, then open it again. The version is shown at the bottom of the ☰ menu.
  • My tapes, drawings or notes have disappeared (Web version). They are kept in the browser, with the notes' photos: they are erased along with the site's browsing data, and do not follow you from one device to another. Export what matters: a drawing is saved as a file from My drawings, and all your data at once with Export my data, in the menu.
  • The plan stays a sketch, or the PDF says so. Measurements are missing: enter the one for each side (only one side per direction can be missing: it is worked out). If the measurements in one direction do not agree, the screen gives the difference to correct.
  • A photo is attached as a file. The calculator reads JPEG and PNG photos. An iPhone HEIC photo copied to a computer, for example, is attached as a file: attach it from the phone instead, which gives it as a JPEG.
